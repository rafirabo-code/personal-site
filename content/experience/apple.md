**Chip design.** My main focus was chip design. I drove several products, especially in SSD and
networking, that shipped across Apple devices: Macs, iPhones and Apple Watches. I led the first and
second generations of a multi-purpose IP block, worked on 11 chip designs, and delivered every one on
time or early.

**The tool.** Then I moved to build a new tool, combining software and hardware, to monitor Apple chips
in depth. It meant bringing several teams together, defining what the tool should do and how it should
feel, and characterizing the product end to end. That was my first real product work.
