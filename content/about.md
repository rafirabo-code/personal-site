I'm a product manager. I've been a founder, and I trained as an electrical engineer.

I served in special forces, studied electrical engineering at Tel Aviv University,
and spent five years designing chips at Apple. Building a tool for those chips pulled me toward product.

At Microsoft I moved from Surface to Azure to Dynamics 365, led the 0→1 launch of Copilot for Sales,
then led agent evaluation in Copilot Studio. Then I left to start Batta and then Penny. Neither worked
out, and they taught me more than any job had.

I grew up 200 meters from the beach in Kiryat Yam, and now live in Tel Aviv. I love surfing, cooking and
baking, reading and writing.
