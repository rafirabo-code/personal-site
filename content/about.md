I'm a product manager. I've been a founder, and I trained as an electrical engineer.

I served in special forces, studied electrical engineering at Tel Aviv University,
and spent five years as a chip design engineer at Apple. Building a tool for those chips pulled me toward product.

then established the agent evaluation framework in Copilot Studio. Then I spent 8 months as a startup
founder: I built and stopped two ventures, Batta and Penny. I learned more than in any job.

I grew up 200 meters from the beach in Kiryat Yam, and now live in Tel Aviv. Outside of work: surfing
since childhood, cooking, baking, reading and writing.
